SIGNAL is an AI-powered forensic trust-intelligence platform that analyzes suspicious online content, including job offers, URLs, emails and forwarded messages.

It explains not only **what** may be risky, but **why** — through contextual analysis and evidence-based risk assessment.

**Dual-pass analysis:** AI semantic analysis + rule-based heuristic checks → risk assessment.

🟢 LOW RISK &nbsp;|&nbsp; 🟡 SUSPICIOUS &nbsp;|&nbsp; 🔴 HIGH RISK

**Features:** Multilingual Support • Comparison Mode • Investigation History • AI-Assisted Investigation
